About Netziah // 2026

Netziah helps companies turn real interest into real revenue.

We step into the sales process where most teams lose control: qualifying opportunities, managing follow-up, supporting trained sales personnel, and keeping serious conversations moving until they become closed deals.

Sales is not just about generating interest.
It's about what happens after.
That's why Netziah exists.

The Origin

Meet Our
Founder

"Every conversation is a chance to win. Most companies lose it in what happens next."

Joshua Pineda founded Netziah after working across sales, marketing agencies, attorney-led businesses, high-ticket closing, and business consulting. Across different industries, he saw the same problem: companies were creating interest, but growth was being slowed down by owner-dependent sales, scattered teams, manual follow-up, and disconnected execution.

Netziah was built to solve that gap by bringing the commercial operation together into one structured team. From marketing support and qualification to openers, closers, follow-up, and revenue operations, Netziah helps businesses handle more volume, create accountability, and grow with control.
